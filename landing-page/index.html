THE C.A.R.E. PARTNERSHIP METHOD

A thoughtful way
back to partnership.

A structured approach to understanding emotional patterns, changing the way you communicate, and making room for connection within demanding lives. Four areas of focus, shaped around your relationship.

C / CLARIFY

Understand the drift.

Identify recurring conflict, unmet expectations, competing priorities, and the places where connection has faded.

A / ADDRESS

Explore emotional patterns.

Recognise triggers, habitual responses, emotional needs, and boundaries with greater awareness.

R / REBUILD

Work as partners.

Practise honest conversations, constructive conflict, shared expectations, and decisions made as a team.

E / ESTABLISH

Make connection a practice.

Create realistic rituals for quality time, affection, and emotional check-ins that fit your lives.

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

You can start with a question.

Can I reach out if my partner isn’t ready?

Yes. Sugita works with individuals as well as couples. You can begin by exploring your own experiences, needs, and responses without waiting for your partner to join.

How do I find out about sessions?

Message Sugita on Instagram with the word HOME. Ask about availability, fees, the session format, and what you would like support with before deciding to book.

Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is not a substitute for medical care, psychotherapy, or mental health treatment. If you need that kind of support, seek a qualified professional. If there is abuse, coercion, or danger in a relationship, prioritise your safety and specialist support.

Do I need to have read the book first?

You can reach out directly. The book is another way to become familiar with Sugita’s approach and begin reflecting at your own pace.
